Evidence to systems

Shared evidence and staff input become strategy working groups, initiatives, and the systems that make strategy repeatable.

Six stages

How the office builds the plan

Each stage tightens the record: collect, interview, discuss, assess, synthesize, then implement.

01

Planning Notebook

Collect shared files, data, and inputs.

  • Existing plans
  • Data & dashboards
  • Opportunity notes
02

Program Manager Interviews

Review past planning and improvements.

  • Lessons learned
  • Process gaps
  • Cross-cutting needs
03

Team Planning Sessions

Discuss current efforts and feedback.

  • Work underway
  • Initiative management
  • Partnership needs
04

Operational Effectiveness

Assess operations with the Director and MPAs.

  • Process health
  • Decision flow
  • Support gaps
05

Synthesis & Retreat

Convert insights into a draft plan.

  • Strategy retreat
  • Draft plan
  • Feedback cycle
06

Systems Implementation

Make strategy repeatable.

  • Project management
  • Strategic initiatives
  • Partnerships
Takeaway

The redesign starts with evidence, staff input, and office-wide synthesis.
